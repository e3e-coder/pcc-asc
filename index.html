About Us

The Academic Support Club at Pasadena City College is a student-led community dedicated to helping students succeed academically, discover transfer opportunities, connect with campus resources, and build meaningful connections with other PCC students.

🎓 Transfer Support

Learn about UC and CSU transfer requirements, TAG, UC TAP, ASSIST, application timelines, and transfer preparation.

📚 Academic Resources

Discover tutoring, counseling, academic planning, scholarships, campus programs, and other opportunities available to PCC students.

🤝 Student Community

Meet other students, exchange advice, build friendships, and become part of a supportive academic community.

💡 Workshops

Attend transfer workshops, student panels, academic success events, and other club activities.
